# Ember Siege Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 13, 2026_

This policy explains how **Ember Siege** (the "game"), published by Defacer Studios, handles information on Android (Google Play). The short version: the game doesn't collect personal information itself. Google services built into the game may collect some information as described below.

## Information the game stores on your device
The game saves the following in a small file on your device:
- **Progress:** records such as the longest night survived, most foes banished and dawns reached, the embers you've earned and spent, shop upgrades, the keepers you own, the maps you've unlocked and the achievements you've earned in the game
- **Missions and Codex:** your mission progress and finished missions, and the foes, weapons and evolutions you've discovered
- **Daily Night results:** your survival time for each day's ranked Daily Night, for up to the last 30 days
- **Hints:** which first-run tips you've already seen
- **Choices and settings:** your last map, keeper, relic and curses, the lantern and cloak you've chosen, and your settings (sound, music, screen shake, damage numbers, reduced flashing, colorblind-safe shots and fullscreen)

None of this identifies you, and the file never leaves your device, except through the services described below that you choose to use. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Google Play Games Services
If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game uses it to:
- unlock **achievements** and post your scores to **leaderboards**
- post your first Daily Night time each day to the **Daily Night** leaderboard
- store your progress in a **saved game** in your Google account, so it syncs between your devices. This copy holds your records, embers, shop upgrades, keepers, unlocked maps, achievements, mission progress, Codex discoveries, seen hints and Daily Night results. Your settings, cosmetic choices and last relic and curse picks stay on each device and are not uploaded.

Google processes this information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage or delete your Play Games data at any time in your Google account and in the Play Games app. Signing in is optional, and the game works fully without it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game can show **rewarded ads**, but only when you choose to watch one (for example, to revive, reroll a level-up, double your embers or claim the daily gift). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use:
- your device's advertising ID
- approximate location (derived from IP address)
- device and app information, diagnostics, and how you interact with ads

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Security
Information sent by the Google services above is encrypted in transit. The game itself sends no data to the developer.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

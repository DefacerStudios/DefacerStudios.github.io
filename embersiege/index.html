Ember Siege

Survive the night. Free on Android.

The night is long, and the shadows are hungry. You hold the last lantern. Drag anywhere to move; your weapons fire on their own. Pick a gift at every level, evolve your weapons and keep the light burning until dawn.

Coming soon to Google Play

What's inside

Five haunted maps

Ashen Field, Drowned Marsh, Frozen Ruins, Withered Orchard and Cinder Mines, each with its own boss and hazards.

Build your keeper

Eleven weapons, eleven evolutions and three hidden fusions. Six keepers, each with a different start.

Every night is new

Arcana cards, curses and relics change each run. A fresh Daily Night every day, with a leaderboard.

Eclipse

Reached dawn on a map? Its Eclipse is a harder night for richer embers.

Google Play Games

25 achievements, 4 leaderboards and cloud saves that keep your progress across devices.

Fair and optional ads

No forced ads. Watch one only if you want a revive, a reroll, double embers or the daily gift.

---
title: Ember Siege is coming to Google Play
---
Our first game, **Ember Siege**, is in closed testing and coming soon to Google Play.

You hold the last lantern through a ten-minute night. Shadows pour out of the dark, your weapons fire on their own, and every level brings a new gift. Keep the light burning until dawn.

- Five haunted maps, each with its own boss
- Eleven weapons, eleven evolutions and three hidden fusions
- Six keepers, curses, relics and Arcana cards
- A new Daily Night every day, with a leaderboard
- Free, with only optional ads

[See the game page](/embersiege/) for screenshots and details. We'll post here the day it goes live.
